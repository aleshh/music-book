# About the author {.unnumbered .matter}

Jonathan Romanovský is a generated authorial persona, not a biographical
claim. He was created to give this book a consistent working voice: that of a
patient music theorist, practicing composer, improviser, and teacher who is
skeptical of easy rules and keeps returning abstract ideas to what can actually
be heard.

The manuscript was developed through an extended collaboration between Alesh
Houdek and an AI large language model. Houdek conceived and directed the book,
selected its structure and examples, supplied objections and new material,
guided successive revisions, and wrote the Foreword. The model generated the
prose within that human-directed process. Romanovský is the name given to the
resulting authorial voice, not a person presented as fact.

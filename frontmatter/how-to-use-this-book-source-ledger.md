# Source Ledger: How to use this book

This file documents the factual precedent behind the usage note. It is not
included in the book build.

- Claim: Brian Eno and Peter Schmidt first published *Oblique Strategies*
  together in 1975 after independently developing related collections of
  working propositions.
- Sources: Eno's first-person 1978 interview; Geeta Dayal's Rhizome history;
  official Eno Shop listing.
  https://www.interviewmagazine.com/music/new-again-brian-eno
  https://old.rhizome.org/editorial/2009/oct/21/brian-eno-peter-schmidt-and-cybernetics/
  https://enoshop.co.uk/products/oblique-strategies
- Confidence: HIGH.

- Claim: Eno drew a card when a piece was stalled or when several routes seemed
  equally plausible. He described the cards as interruptions of narrowed,
  linear thought under pressure rather than sources of finished musical
  material.
- Sources: Eno's 1978 interview and Dayal's account of Eno's 1980 KPFA
  interview, above.
- Confidence: HIGH.

- Interpretation: The book's 36 interventions can perform a related function
  while adding musical explanation and explicit limitations. This is the
  manuscript's own comparison, not a claim made by Eno or Schmidt.

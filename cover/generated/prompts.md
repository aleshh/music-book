# Generated cover-art prompts

These prompts produced the three text-free concept artworks in this directory.
Typography is intentionally reserved for Figma.

## Variant 1: Process and drift

Create a text-free front-cover artwork for a compact 4.25 x 7 inch paperback
field guide about composing ambient and minimalist music.

**Visual concept — process and drift:** An abstract editorial composition made
from a restricted vocabulary of very fine horizontal rules, tiny bars, sparse
dots, and a few translucent paper-like planes. The marks begin with lucid
order, then gradually fall out of alignment through subtle phase drift,
accumulation, omission, and return. The image should make limited musical
material feel consequential: repetition with memory, gradual change, breath,
and one or two small disruptions. Include one restrained charcoal event that
becomes a focal point without dominating.

**Style:** Mid-century experimental-music program meets contemporary
independent book design; tactile risograph and letterpress character on warm
off-white uncoated paper; slight ink misregistration and human irregularity,
but clean and deliberate rather than distressed. Quietly intellectual,
inviting, practical, and alive. Palette limited to carbon-copy-paper colors:
pale mint green, warm pale yellow, dusty pink, charcoal, and paper white.

**Composition:** Tall portrait artwork, approximately 4.25:7 proportion, full
bleed. Strong asymmetry and generous negative space. Keep a calm, low-detail
zone slightly right of center and in the lower-middle portion for typography to
be added later. The composition must still work when reduced to a small Amazon
thumbnail.

**Do not include:** No words, letters, numbers, notation, staff paper, musical
notes, waveforms, synthesizers, headphones, landscapes, sunsets, clouds,
galaxies, gradients, concentric rectangles, book mockup, spine, barcode, or
photographic objects. No glossy 3D effects. Output only the flat front-cover
artwork.

## Variant 2: Resonance and attention

Create a text-free front-cover artwork for a compact 4.25 x 7 inch paperback
field guide about composing ambient and minimalist music.

**Visual concept — resonance and attention:** A spare abstract resonance map:
three or four large, soft-edged elliptical or bowed forms cross a field of
hairline rules. Each form appears to set the next one gently in motion, like
sustained tones changing the way nearby silence is heard. Overlaps create new
muted colors; a few fine interruptions suggest breath, decay, acoustics, and
the listener's shifting attention. It should feel like an experimental score
crossed with an archival scientific plate, but it must not resemble
conventional music notation or a literal diagram.

**Style:** Sophisticated independent-press book design; tactile monoprint,
translucent carbon-copy paper, dry pencil, and precise ink on warm off-white
uncoated stock. Restrained, materially rich, quietly strange, and inviting
rather than mystical. Palette limited to pale mint green, warm pale yellow,
dusty pink, charcoal, and paper white.

**Composition:** Tall portrait artwork, approximately 4.25:7 proportion, full
bleed. Forms should be placed asymmetrically with generous breathing room.
Reserve a calm, low-detail vertical area slightly right of center, especially
from the middle to lower-middle, where typography will be added later. Maintain
a strong graphic silhouette that reads as a small online thumbnail.

**Do not include:** No words, letters, numbers, notation, musical notes, staff
lines, waveforms, instruments, headphones, landscapes, clouds, water,
galaxies, glowing light effects, concentric rectangles, book mockup, spine,
barcode, or photographic objects. No glossy 3D rendering. Output only the flat
front-cover artwork.

## Variant 3: Developing variation

Create a text-free front-cover artwork for a compact 4.25 x 7 inch paperback
field guide about composing ambient and minimalist music.

**Visual concept — developing variation:** Build the image from one extremely
simple printed module—an imperfect short rounded bar or small paper
lozenge—repeated in a loose vertical sequence. Each recurrence changes by only
one audible-looking variable: length, spacing, register on the page, opacity,
edge, or color. Some forms nearly repeat, one disappears, one returns altered,
and one slips sideways. The sequence should demonstrate how tiny compositional
choices create form, without becoming a rigid grid or decorative pattern. It
should suggest a practical exercise laid out by an exact but playful composer.

**Style:** Contemporary small-press cover with the physical character of cut
paper, carbon-copy sheets, rubber-stamp ink, and restrained risograph
misregistration on warm off-white uncoated stock. Crisp enough to feel
designed, imperfect enough to feel performed. Palette limited to pale mint
green, warm pale yellow, dusty pink, charcoal, and paper white. Intelligent,
energetic, humane, and slightly dry-humored.

**Composition:** Tall portrait artwork, approximately 4.25:7 proportion, full
bleed. The repeated modules travel mainly along the left third and upper edge,
occasionally entering the center; leave a generous calm zone slightly right of
center and through the lower-middle for title typography to be added later.
Use scale and placement boldly enough to read at thumbnail size. Avoid a
symmetrical grid.

**Do not include:** No words, letters, numbers, notation, musical notes, staff
paper, waveforms, instruments, headphones, scenery, clouds, galaxies,
gradients, concentric rectangles, book mockup, spine, barcode, or photographic
objects. No glossy 3D effects. Output only the flat front-cover artwork.
